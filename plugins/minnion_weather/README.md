# Weather

The weather now, highs and lows, rain, wind, air quality and more.

The weather for your home town (or any town) on your keys: now with a matching picture, today's high and low, feels like, chance of rain, humidity, wind, air quality, tomorrow, and the next sunrise or sunset. Updated every 15 minutes; press a key to hear it in full. Weather data by Open-Meteo.com, free and with no account.

Open **Marketplace** in MinnionDeck and press **Install**, or tick it in the MinnionDeck setup. Its profile is added with it.

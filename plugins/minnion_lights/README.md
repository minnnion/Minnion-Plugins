# Case Lights

Finds out why your PC's case lights act up, and sets them for you.

Checks the PC's lighting every 5 minutes and when you ask: what changed the lights, which apps fight over them, and how to fix it. Turns the lights on, off or to a colour from your keys, and can follow the colours on your screen.

## Install

Open **Marketplace** in MinnionDeck and press **Install**, or tick it in the MinnionDeck setup. Its profile is added with it.

## Actions

- Lights status
- Lights acted up
- Set the lights
- Screen sync
- Fix a lights issue
- Lights report
- Lights timeline

## Settings

- How far back to look
- Watch the lighting controller
- Keep the lights as I set them
- Clear my logged moments

## You need

- A board with RGB lighting (MSI Mystic Light is supported best)

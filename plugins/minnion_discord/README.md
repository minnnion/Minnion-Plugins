# Discord

Mute, deafen, camera, screen share, push to talk and leave call, live.

Controls Discord's voice from your deck, and each key shows Discord's live state: mute your mic, deafen, turn your camera on or off, share your screen, switch push to talk and voice activity, and leave the call. Keep Discord open; the first time, Discord asks you to Authorize MinnionDeck, and after that it connects by itself.

## Install

Open **Marketplace** in MinnionDeck and press **Install**, or tick it in the MinnionDeck setup. Its profile is added with it.

- Mute mic
- Deafen
- Camera
- Screen share
- Leave call

# Spotify

Play, skip, like, volume and now playing.

Controls Spotify from your deck and shows what's playing on a key. You sign in once in your browser and MinnionDeck keeps you signed in.

## Install

Open **Marketplace** in MinnionDeck and press **Install**, or tick it in the MinnionDeck setup. Its profile is added with it.

## Actions

- Now playing
- Previous song
- Next song
- Play or pause
- Like the song
- Spotify volume
- Spotify library

## Settings

- Spotify Client ID
- Sign in
- Log out

## You need

- A Spotify account
- Your own Client ID from developer.spotify.com (free, takes a minute)
